03 · BREWED THE TAPRI WAY

Bhatti se kulhad tak,char asool.

Forty saal mein sirf ek hi cheez badli — kulhad ka size. Baaki sab as-is.

01

Bhatti at dawn

Coal sigri lit at 5 AM sharp. Brass bartan mein Assam CTC, taaza khoya doodh, aur gud ke dane. Gas stove se chai ka swaad bhaag jaata hai — ye humara pehla asool hai.

भट्टी
02

The do-ubaal rule

Chai is boiled twice — ubaalo, utaro, phir ubaalo. Never rushed, never reheated. Jaldi wali chai ghar pe piyo; yahan patience hi asli masala hai.

दो उबाल
03

Masala kut-ta hai roz

Adrak, elaichi, laung, kali mirch — sab subah sil-batte pe haath se kuta jaata hai. Packet masala tapri mein 1987 mein aaya tha aur wahin ruk gaya.
